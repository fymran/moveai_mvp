# Video editing and delivery plan

## Edit sequence

1. Select a clean take that follows the seven timed scenes in `video_script.md` and stays within 4:30–5:00.
2. Trim only dead air, accidental clicks, and long navigation pauses. Preserve the real application flow and result values.
3. Add a simple opening title: **Ubiquitous MoveAI — Synthetic Shared Shuttle Routing Prototype**.
4. Keep the persistent or repeated disclosure **Synthetic simulation — illustrative inputs, not measured results** visible during metric scenes.
5. Add accurate burned-in captions. Check every metric label, unit, proper name, and constraint against the app.
6. Use restrained cuts or fades. Avoid stock footage, maps, logos, animated route overlays, or sound effects that could be mistaken for real service evidence.
7. End on the dashboard and a concise next-step statement: validate demand and operating assumptions with local stakeholders.

## Quality check

- Runtime is 4:30–5:00 and the first 10 seconds identify the project and synthetic nature of the demo.
- Narration is intelligible, balanced, and synchronized; captions match it.
- Text and route details are readable at normal playback size.
- Every numerical claim matches `results_to_capture.md` and the captured app run.
- No unsupported claims about research, measured performance, local demand, customers, adoption, costs, emissions, or real-world savings.
- No Streamlit error screen, personal notifications, or unrelated desktop content is visible.
- Play the exported file from beginning to end, including audio, on a second playback if possible.

## Export and handoff

- Export MP4 using H.264 video and AAC audio, 1920×1080, 30 fps, with a broadly compatible profile.
- Use a descriptive filename such as `Ubiquitous_MoveAI_Hackathon_Demo.mp4`.
- Keep a high-quality master and a delivery copy; verify the exported file opens and that its duration, audio, and captions are correct.
- Upload the final MP4 to the organizer-approved hosting platform. Set link access so hackathon reviewers can view it without requesting access, then test the link in a signed-out/private browser window.
- Add the final video URL to the submission form/deck as required. Keep the repository URL and video URL distinct and verify both before submission.
- Do not mark a video as delivered or provide a hosting link until the MP4 has actually been rendered, uploaded, and link-tested.
